FAQ

Tour Packages from Bangalore — FAQs

Everything you need to know about booking tour packages from Bangalore with Khushi Travels.

Does Khushi Travels offer tour packages from Bangalore? +

Yes. We offer custom tour packages from Bangalore across India — pilgrimage, hill stations, beaches, heritage, wildlife, and adventure destinations. We handle transport (own fleet 9-50 seater), hotel booking (Gold & Platinum tiers), and tour guide arrangements (Platinum, for 15+ groups). We've served 500+ tour customers since 2018.

What pilgrimage tour packages from Bangalore do you offer? +

We offer pilgrimage tour packages from Bangalore to — Tirupati (₹9,500, 2D/1N), Shirdi (₹22,000, 4D/3N), Palani (₹12,500, 3D/2N), Dharmasthala (₹8,500, 2D/1N), Rameswaram (₹18,500, 4D/3N), Srisailam (₹14,500, 3D/2N), Puri Jagannath (₹35,000, 5D/4N), and Varanasi (₹38,000, 5D/4N). All packages include comfortable vehicle, professional driver, and fuel.

Do you provide hotel booking with your tour packages from Bangalore? +

Yes. Our Gold package includes vehicle + hotel booking (3★ or 4★ verified hotels). Our Platinum package includes vehicle + hotel + dedicated tour guide for groups of 15+. All hotels are pre-verified for quality, cleanliness, and location. The Silver package is transport-only if you already have accommodation arranged.

What vehicles are available for tour packages from Bangalore? +

Depending on your group size — 9 seater luxury tempo traveller (Maharaja pushback seats, LED TV, USB), 12-14 seater tempo traveller (pushback seats, panoramic windows), Force Urbania (air suspension, standing height, panoramic windows), 16 seater tempo traveller, 20 seater mini bus, 35-50 seater luxury bus, and Toyota Innova Crysta for smaller groups. All 100% owned, sanitized, and GPS-tracked.

How far in advance should I book a tour from Bangalore? +

We recommend booking 7-14 days in advance for popular destinations like Coorg, Ooty, Goa, and Tirupati. During peak season (November-February), 3-4 weeks advance booking is advised for preferred hotels and vehicle availability. However, we can arrange last-minute tours with 24-48 hours notice for most destinations — just WhatsApp us.

Can I customize my tour package from Bangalore? +

Absolutely. Share your destination, travel dates, group size, budget, and preferred activities. We'll design a custom itinerary with the perfect vehicle, route, halts, meals, and accommodation — all within your budget. We specialise in corporate retreats, student educational tours, family holidays, and pilgrimage groups. WhatsApp us for a free quote.
