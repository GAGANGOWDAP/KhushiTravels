FAQ

Pricing FAQs

How much does a 12 seater tempo traveller cost in Bangalore? +

A luxury 12 seater tempo traveller in Bangalore starts at ₹28/km with Maharaja pushback seats. Standard 12 seater starts at ₹19/km. All include fuel, AC, driver, and 24/7 support.

What is included in the per km price? +

The per km price includes fuel, vehicle rental, AC charges, and driver salary. Driver allowance (₹400-₹1000/day) is charged separately. Tolls, parking, and state entry taxes are extra as per actuals.

Is there a minimum distance for outstation trips? +

Yes, minimum 300 km per day applies for outstation trips. For local moves within Bangalore, we offer hourly packages starting from 8 hours / 80 km.

Do you offer one-way drops? +

Yes, we offer one-way drops to destinations like Mysore, Tirupati, Chennai, and more. You only pay for the distance traveled with no return charge. Contact us for a custom quote.

Are there any hidden charges? +

Absolutely none. We believe in transparent pricing. The per km rate, driver allowance, and extra charges (toll/parking) are clearly communicated before booking. What we quote is what you pay.
