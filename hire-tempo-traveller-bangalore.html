Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & Reservation FAQs

Answers to common questions regarding quotes, advances, and rental policies.

How do I book a tempo traveller in Bangalore with Khushi Travels?

Booking is simple and takes under 2 minutes. Click our WhatsApp button or call +91 99013 33113 with your preferred vehicle, destination, travel dates, and group size. We reply with a detailed per-km quotation within 5 minutes. A 50% advance confirms your booking, and the balance is payable before trip start.

What information is needed to receive an accurate quote?

To provide you with an exact tariff, please share: your starting locality in Bangalore, final destination(s), departure and return dates, approximate passenger count, and preferred vehicle model (e.g. 12 seater Maharaja, 16 seater, or Force Urbania). We will calculate total estimated kilometres and driver charges transparently.

What are the payment and confirmation terms?

We require a 50% advance payment at the time of booking confirmation to lock your vehicle and assign a chauffeur. The remaining 50% balance must be cleared before the trip start date. We accept UPI, bank transfers, and standard digital payment methods with GST invoices provided.

Do you provide airport transfers and Bangalore local rentals?

Yes. In addition to outstation tours, we offer 24/7 Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) pickup and drop-off packages, corporate group transportation, wedding guest logistics, and local 8hr/80km or 12hr/120km Bangalore city sightseeing rentals.
