12 Jyotirlingas Covered

Sacred Shrines of India

From the snowy peaks of Kedarnath to the shores of Rameshwaram — explore all 12 Jyotirlingas in one grand pilgrimage.

01

Somnath

First Jyotirlinga. Associated with the Moon God Chandra. Located on the western coast of Gujarat.

02

Nageshwar

Known as Daruka-Vana, the forest of divine trees. A symbol of protection against poisons.

03

Trimbakeshwar

Unique three-faced Linga. Source of the Godavari River. One of the most sacred pilgrimage sites.

04

Bhimashankar

Source of the Bhima River. Nestled in the Sahyadri hills of the Western Ghats.

05

Grishneshwar

Near UNESCO Ellora Caves. The final Jyotirlinga according to the Shiva Purana.

06

Mahakaleshwar

Only self-born (Svayambhu) Jyotirlinga. The Linga faces south. Famous Bhasmabhisheka ritual.

07

Omkareshwar

Situated on an Om-shaped island in the Narmada River. Spiritually charged location.

08

Kashi Vishwanath

The most famous Shiva temple. Gold-plated spire on the banks of the Ganges.

09

Kedarnath

Northernmost Jyotirlinga. Part of Char Dham at 3,583m in the snow-capped Himalayas.

10

Vaidyanath

Associated with the Ravana legend. Famous Shravan Mela festival.

11

Mallikarjuna

Only site where a Jyotirlinga and Shakti Peetha exist together.

12

Rameshwaram

Southernmost Jyotirlinga. Installed by Lord Rama. Famous 1,000-pillar corridor.

23-Day Itinerary

Day-by-Day Journey Plan

A carefully crafted 23-day pilgrimage from Bengaluru covering all 12 Jyotirlingas across 8+ states of India.

1

Bengaluru → Gujarat

Days 1–4

Day 1

Departure

Evening departure from Bengaluru by luxury Volvo bus.

Day 2

Somnath

Arrive Somnath. Darshan at Somnath Jyotirlinga — the first Jyotirlinga.

Day 3

Nageshwar

Visit Nageshwar Jyotirlinga (Dwarka). Evening travel towards Maharashtra.

Day 4

Travel Day

Scenic journey from Gujarat to Maharashtra. Overnight stay en route.

2

Maharashtra

Days 5–7

Day 5

Trimbakeshwar

Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga darshan. Source of Godavari River. Unique three-faced Linga.

Day 6

Bhimashankar

Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga darshan in the Sahyadri hills. Overnight stay.

Day 7

Grishneshwar

Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga near Ellora Caves. Travel to Madhya Pradesh.

3

Madhya Pradesh

Days 8–10

Day 8

Mahakaleshwar

Ujjain. Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga — the only south-facing, self-born Jyotirlinga.

Day 9

Omkareshwar

Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga on an Om-shaped island in the Narmada River.

Day 10

Travel Day

Journey from MP to Uttar Pradesh. Overnight travel.

4

Uttar Pradesh

Days 11–13

Day 11

Varanasi Arrival

Arrive Varanasi. Evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Day 12

Kashi Vishwanath

Special darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga — the golden-spired temple.

Day 13

Travel to UK

Scenic journey from Varanasi to Uttarakhand. Overnight stay en route.

5

Uttarakhand (Himalayas)

Days 14–16

Day 14

Kedarnath Trek

Journey to Kedarnath. Helicopter/trek to the 3,583m high shrine.

Day 15

Kedarnath Darshan

Special darshan at Kedarnath Jyotirlinga. Return to base camp.

Day 16

Travel East

Journey from Uttarakhand to Jharkhand. Overnight travel.

6

Jharkhand → Andhra → Tamil Nadu → Return

Days 17–23

Day 17

Vaidyanath

Deoghar. Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga darshan. Associated with the Ravana legend.

Day 18-19

Travel South

Long journey from Jharkhand to Andhra Pradesh. Overnight travel with rest stops.

Day 20

Mallikarjuna

Srisailam. Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga — unique Jyotirlinga cum Shakti Peetha.

Day 21

Travel to TN

Journey from Andhra to Tamil Nadu. Scenic route through eastern ghats.

Day 22

Rameshwaram

Rameshwaram Jyotirlinga — installed by Lord Rama. Famous 1,000-pillar corridor.

Day 23

🏁 Return

Journey back to Bengaluru. Arrive evening with cherished memories of a lifetime.
