Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 12 seater tempo traveller cost in Bangalore? +

A luxury 12 seater tempo traveller in Bangalore starts at ₹28/km with Maharaja pushback seats. Standard 12 seater starts at ₹18/km. All prices include fuel, AC, professional driver, and 24/7 support. No hidden charges.

How many passengers can a 13 seater tempo traveller accommodate? +

A 13 seater tempo traveller accommodates 13 passengers plus the driver with pushback reclining seats and ample legroom for every passenger. Available as a luxury variant at ₹35/km. Perfect for large families, wedding groups, and corporate outings.

Does the 12 seater tempo have pushback seats? +

Yes. Our luxury 12 seater tempo travellers feature Maharaja pushback seats with 1x1 configuration — every passenger gets aisle access. Features include individual AC vents, USB charging ports, 21-inch LED TV, and premium leather upholstery. The seats recline fully for maximum comfort on long journeys.

What is the difference between 12 seater and 14 seater tempo traveller? +

A 12 seater tempo traveller offers Maharaja pushback seats in a 1x1 configuration with maximum legroom and luxury — ideal for smaller groups who prioritize comfort. A 14 seater adds two more seats with a wider body, offering more capacity while still maintaining pushback comfort. Choose 12 seater for ultra-luxury with extra legroom, or 14 seater if you need to accommodate a slightly larger group.
