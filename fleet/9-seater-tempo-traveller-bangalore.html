Does the 9 seater tempo have Maharaja seats? +

Yes! Our 9 seater luxury tempo travellers feature Maharaja pushback seats in 1x1 configuration — meaning every passenger gets an individual seat with its own armrest and window. No middle seats. Features include individual AC vents, USB charging at every seat, a 21-inch LED TV, and premium leather upholstery. The Ultra Luxury variant adds larger screens, subwoofer, and wireless charging.

What is the difference between 9 seater and 10 seater tempo traveller? +

A 9 seater tempo traveller offers Maharaja pushback seats in 1x1 row configuration with maximum legroom and luxury — ideal for 8 passengers plus driver. A 10 seater (Force Urbania) adds one extra seat in the last row while still offering pushback seats and premium amenities. If you value maximum per-person comfort choose the 9 seater; if you need one more seat choose the Urbania.
