FAQ

Outstation Trip FAQs

What is the minimum km for outstation trips? +

Minimum 300 km per day applies for all outstation trips from Bangalore. For multi-day trips, the minimum applies per day. Nearby destinations like Mysore (150 km one-way) can be done under our one-way drop service with no minimum km requirement.

Do you offer one-way drops from Bangalore? +

Yes. One-way drops are available to Mysore, Tirupati, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pondicherry, Mangalore, and many more destinations. You pay only for the distance traveled — no return charge. The vehicle drops you and returns empty. Ideal for pilgrimage, dropping someone at the airport/railway station, or one-way relocation.

What vehicles are available for outstation trips? +

All our vehicles are available for outstation — luxury tempo travellers (9, 12, 14, 16 seater with Maharaja pushback seats), Force Urbania (9-16 seater), Innova Crysta, luxury cars (Ertiga, Dzire), mini buses (20-26 seater), and full-size luxury buses (35-50 seater). 100% owned fleet, no third-party vehicles.

What is included in the outstation price? +

The per-km price includes fuel, AC, driver salary, and comprehensive insurance. Additional charges: driver allowance (batta — ₹400/day for cars, ₹600/day for tempo travellers, ₹1000/day for buses), tolls, parking fees, and state entry taxes. Night halt allowance of ₹1000 applies if the driver stays overnight.

How to book an outstation tempo traveller? +

Booking is simple. Just message us on WhatsApp at +91 99013 33113 with your destination, travel dates, and group size. We'll confirm availability and share exact pricing within 5 minutes. Payment options: cash, UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, credit/debit card, and bank transfer. Advance booking recommended during holiday seasons.
