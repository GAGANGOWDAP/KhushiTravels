## DECISION: SHOW ADDRESS vs HIDE ADDRESS

For a tempo traveller rental business with a physical office:

### Option A: Show Address (RECOMMENDED)
✅ You have a real physical office with signage
✅ Website prominently shows the address
✅ Easier to verify with utility bills
✅ More trust with customers
⚠️ Requires permanent signage matching business name at location
⚠️ Office must be staffed during stated hours

### Option B: Keep Hidden (Service-Area Only)
✅ Less documentation needed for appeal
⚠️ Must remove ALL address mentions from description
⚠️ Service area must be reasonable (≤2 hrs from base)
⚠️ Harder to verify legitimacy with Google

**→ RECOMMENDATION: Option A (Show Address)**
Your business has a real office. Hiding it while your website shows it creates the inconsistency that triggered the suspension.

## APPEAL PROCESS

### Step 1: Apply All Fixes Above

Edit your GMB listing (even while suspended, you can edit):
1. Go to https://business.google.com/
2. Select your suspended profile
3. Apply ALL fixes from Steps 1-8 above
4. Wait 24-48 hours before appealing (let Google's system see the changes)

### Step 2: Submit Appeal

Go to: https://support.google.com/business/answer/4569145

1. Click "Submit an appeal"
2. Select the suspended profile
3. Write a brief, factual appeal note:

```
Dear Google Business Profile Team,

Our Business Profile for Khushi Tours And Travels (Bengaluru) was recently suspended. We have reviewed Google's guidelines and made the following corrections to ensure full compliance:

1. Corrected business address to match our physical office location and website
2. Updated business categories to accurately represent our core service
3. Removed business hours in compliance with transportation service guidelines
4. Narrowed service area to reasonable driving distance from our office
5. Updated business description with factual, non-promotional content

We are a legitimate tour operator and tempo traveller rental service operating from our registered office at Kodigehalli Main Road, Vidyaranyapura, Bengaluru since August 2018. Our website (khushitravel.in) has consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data matching this profile.

Attached please find supporting documents:
- GST registration certificate
- Office address proof (utility bill)
- Photos of our office with signage
- Business license

We respectfully request reinstatement of our Business Profile. We are committed to maintaining accurate, policy-compliant information going forward.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
Khushi Tours And Travels
+91 99013 33113
```

4. Upload documents (you have 60 minutes after submitting appeal)

### Step 3: Wait — Do NOT Touch

- Response time: 3-14 days typically
- Do NOT make any changes during this period
- Do NOT submit another appeal

### Step 4: If Denied

If your appeal is NOT approved:
- Don't panic — this is common for first appeals
- Wait 30 days before resubmitting
- Gather STRONGER evidence (more photos, video verification)
- Submit via the "Additional Review" option

## KEY GOOGLE GUIDELINES (for reference)

From https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177:

- **Name**: Must reflect real-world name on signage, website, stationery
- **Address**: Must be precise, accurate. No P.O. boxes, no virtual offices
- **Service area**: Max ~2 hours driving from base
- **Categories**: Use fewest needed to describe core business. Don't use as keywords
- **Hours**: Transportation services should NOT provide hours
- **Description**: No links, no promotions, no gimmicky text, factual only
- **Phone**: Must be under direct control of business. Local number preferred
- **Women-owned**: Requires documentation to verify
