FAQ

Bangalore to Mysore Tempo Traveller — FAQs

How far is Mysore from Bangalore?+

Mysore is 150 km from Bangalore. The drive takes 3 hours via NICE Road and Mysore Road (NH-275). It is Karnataka's most popular weekend getaway destination.

How much does a tempo traveller from Bangalore to Mysore cost?+

₹Tempo traveller from Bangalore to Mysore costs ₹19-₹60/km. For a 1-day trip: standard 12 seater ~₹13,300, luxury ~₹19,600, Force Urbania ~₹42,000. Driver allowance ₹600/day extra.

What is the best time to visit Mysore?+

October to February is ideal. Pleasant 20-30°C. Dussehra festival (Sep-Oct) is spectacular. March-May is hot (35°C+). June-September brings moderate rainfall.

Can I do a one-day trip to Mysore by tempo traveller?+

Yes, a one-day Mysore trip is very popular. Depart Bangalore at 6 AM, reach Mysore by 9 AM. Visit Palace, Chamundi Hills, lunch. Brindavan Gardens evening, return by 10 PM.

Which tempo traveller is best for Mysore road trip?+

For a family group, the luxury 12 seater tempo traveller with pushback seats is ideal. For smaller groups, the Toyota Innova Crysta is comfortable and economical.
