FAQ

Travel Blog FAQs

What is the best tempo traveller for group travel from Bangalore?+

The best tempo traveller depends on group size. For 7-9 passengers, a 9 seater luxury tempo traveller with Maharaja seats is ideal. For 12-16 passengers, the Force Urbania offers panoramic windows and air suspension. For 20-50 passengers, luxury buses are the best choice. Khushi Travels offers all options with 100% owned fleet.

How much does a tempo traveller cost per km in Bangalore?+

Tempo traveller rental in Bangalore ranges from ₹18/km (non-AC 12-14 seater) to ₹60/km (luxury Force Urbania 9-12 seater). Prices include fuel, AC, and insurance. Driver allowance and tolls are extra.

Which are the best weekend getaways from Bangalore?+

Top weekend getaways from Bangalore via tempo traveller include: Mysore (2.5 hrs, 150 km), Coorg (5 hrs, 270 km), Ooty (6 hrs, 300 km), Chikmagalur (4 hrs, 245 km), Wayanad (5.5 hrs, 280 km), Hampi (6 hrs, 350 km), and Pondicherry (5 hrs, 310 km).
