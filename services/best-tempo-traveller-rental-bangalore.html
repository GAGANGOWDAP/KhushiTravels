Our Story

The Khushi Travels Story

Founded in 2018 by Suresh J, Khushi Travels started with a simple vision: to redefine group travel in Bangalore. With just one vehicle and a commitment to honesty, Suresh began serving families, pilgrims, and corporate teams with the same care he would his own.

The name "Khushi" means happiness in Hindi — and that's exactly what we deliver. Every journey, every vehicle, every interaction is designed to bring a smile to our customers' faces. Over 8 years, we've grown from a single tempo traveller to a fleet of 50+ vehicles spanning luxury tempo travellers, Force Urbania, premium buses, sedans, and SUVs.

Unlike aggregators who outsource vehicles from third parties, we own every vehicle in our fleet. This means no last-minute switches, no quality compromises, and full accountability. When you book with Khushi Travels, you get exactly what you see — every single time.

Today, we're proud to be Bangalore's most trusted premium tempo traveller rental service, trusted by 5000+ happy groups and rated 4.9★ by our customers. But we're not done yet. We continue to expand our fleet, upgrade our amenities, and improve our service — because at Khushi Travels, every journey matters.
