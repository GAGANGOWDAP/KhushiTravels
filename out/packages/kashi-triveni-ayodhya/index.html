Sacred Yatra Schedule

Detailed 4-Day Pilgrimage Itinerary

Every temple darshan, holy boat ride, and transit leg carefully orchestrated for seamless devotion and senior comfort.

01

DAY 01 – ARRIVAL VARANASI

Kashi Vishwanath Sparsh Darshan • Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti

Distance: 30–40 Kms
  • Arrival at Varanasi Airport / Railway Station.
  • Warm reception and pickup by 20 Seater Luxury Tempo Traveller.
  • Check-in and refresh at Hotel Ananta Kashi, Varanasi.
  • Evening: Divine SPARSH DARSHAN assistance at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga.
  • Darshan at revered Annapurna Mata Temple.
  • Witness the world-famous, awe-inspiring Ganga Maha Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.
  • ★ Evening Boat Ride on River Ganga Included.
  • Dinner and overnight stay at Hotel Ananta Kashi, Varanasi.
02

DAY 02 – VARANASI LOCAL SIGHTSEEING

Subah-E-Banaras • Sarnath Dham • BHU • Sankat Mochan

Distance: 40–50 Kms
  • Early morning: Subah-E-Banaras devotional experience at Assi Ghat.
  • Attend morning Assi Ghat Aarti.
  • ★ Morning Sunrise Boat Ride on River Ganga Included.
  • Return to hotel for breakfast.
  • Excursion to sacred Sarnath Dham (Dhamek Stupa & deer park).
  • Visit the iconic BHU Vishwanath (Birla) Temple.
  • Darshan at holy Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple.
  • Evening free for shopping Banarasi silks and local heritage souvenirs.
  • All meals (Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner) & overnight stay at Hotel Ananta Kashi.
03

DAY 03 – VARANASI – PRAYAGRAJ – AYODHYA

Triveni Sangam Snan • Bade Hanuman Ji • Transit to Ayodhya

~295 Kms / 8 Hours
  • Breakfast and hotel checkout from Varanasi.
  • Scenic highway drive from Varanasi to Prayagraj: 125 Kms / ~3 Hours.
  • Sacred Triveni Sangam Snan at holy confluence of Ganga, Yamuna & Saraswati.
  • ★ Boat Ride for Sangam Snan Included.
  • Darshan at historic Bade Hanuman Ji Temple (reclining Hanuman posture).
  • Visit sacred Alopi Mata Shaktipeeth Temple.
  • Continue highway drive from Prayagraj to Ayodhya: 170 Kms / 4–4.5 Hours.
  • Check-in at Hotel Mosaic, Ayodhya.
  • Dinner and overnight stay at Ayodhya.
04

DAY 04 – AYODHYA – LUCKNOW DEPARTURE

Ram Janmabhoomi Sugam Darshan • Hanuman Garhi • Lucknow Drop

~135 Kms / 3 Hours
  • Morning breakfast at hotel.
  • Divine SUGAM DARSHAN at grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple.
  • ★ Dedicated Golf Cart in Ayodhya Included for seamless darshan.
  • Visit holy Hanuman Garhi fort temple.
  • Darshan at Kanak Bhawan & historic Dashrath Mahal.
  • Peaceful Saryu River Aarti at the river ghats.
  • Afternoon drive from Ayodhya to Lucknow: 135 Kms / ~3 Hours via expressway.
  • Drop at Lucknow Airport / Railway Station with holy prasad & blessed memories.
  • Tour concludes happily with Khushi Tours & Travels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Trip Information & FAQs

What is the booking deposit and payment schedule?

A nominal advance token confirms your 20-seater Tempo Traveller and hotel room blocks. The remaining balance is payable prior to trip departure as outlined in your official B2B invoice.

Can meals be customized for Jain or special diets?

Yes. All meals provided under the APAI plan are pure vegetarian, and special satvik or Jain preferences without onion and garlic can be arranged with prior notification at booking.

How does the temple darshan assistance work?

Our team coordinates Sparsh Darshan protocol assistance at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga and Sugam Darshan with dedicated golf cart transfers at Ayodhya Ram Janmabhoomi to minimize walking fatigue for elders.

What if our group has more or fewer than 16 adults?

The ₹17,900 / person rate is optimized for a group of 16 adults in a 20-seater Tempo Traveller. For custom group sizes, please contact our helpline at +91 99013 33113 for a tailored quote.
