Ooty, Coonoor & Nilgiri Hills Tour

Proposed Tour Itinerary

Customizable for your group
Day 1

Bangalore to Ooty via Bandipur

  • Early morning pickup from Bangalore.
  • Scenic wildlife transit drive through Bandipur and Mudumalai forests.
  • Ascent up the 36 Nilgiri hairpin bends.
  • Check in to hotel, evening walk around Ooty Lake.
Day 2

Ooty & Coonoor Sightseeing

  • Visit Doddabetta Peak for sweeping Nilgiri vistas.
  • Tour the Tea Factory & Chocolate Museum.
  • Drive to Coonoor: visit Sim's Park and Dolphin's Nose viewpoint.
  • Return to Ooty for evening shopping on Commercial Road.
Day 3

Botanical Gardens & Return

  • Morning visit to Government Botanical Garden.
  • Pykara Lake & waterfalls photo stop.
  • Descent towards Bangalore with evening drop.
