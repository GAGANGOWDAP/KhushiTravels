Route & Darshan Schedule

12 Jyotirlinga Day-by-Day Itinerary

A meticulously organized 25-day roadmap covering all 12 Jyotirlingas with adequate rest intervals and darshan timings.

01

Days 1–4: Bengaluru → Gujarat Shrines

Route: Bengaluru → Hubli → Pune → Somnath → Dwarka (Nageshwar).

Evening departure from Bengaluru. Transit to Gujarat coastal highway. Special evening Aarti and morning darshan at Somnath Jyotirlinga (1st Jyotirlinga). Afternoon transit to Dwarka for darshan at Nageshwar Jyotirlinga and Gomti Ghat.

02

Days 5–7: Maharashtra Sacred Trio

Route: Nashik (Trimbak) → Bhimashankar → Aurangabad (Grishneshwar).

Darshan at Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga (source of the Godavari River with unique three-faced linga). Next, transit through the Sahyadri ranges to Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga. Conclude the Maharashtra leg with darshan at Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga near the Ellora Caves.

03

Days 8–10: Madhya Pradesh Shrines

Route: Ujjain → Omkareshwar → Transit towards Uttar Pradesh.

Early morning darshan at Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga in Ujjain (the south-facing Dakshinamoorthy linga). Afternoon visit to Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga located on Mandhata Island in the Narmada River. Evening journey northward.

04

Days 11–13: Uttar Pradesh & Kashi

Route: Varanasi (Kashi) → Sarnath → Transit to Uttarakhand.

Holy dip in the Ganges, special Sugam Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga with golden dome, and evening boat ride witnessing the Dashashwamedh Ghat Maha Aarti. Transit to Haridwar/Rishikesh.

05

Days 14–17: Uttarakhand & Kedarnath Himalayas

Route: Rishikesh → Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath shrine.

Himalayan ascent to Sonprayag. Trek/helicopter transfer to Kedarnath Jyotirlinga (altitude 3,583m). Sacred darshan of the northernmost Jyotirlinga amidst the peaks. Overnight rest at base before descent.

06

Days 18–19: Jharkhand & Baidyanath Dham

Route: Deoghar (Baidyanath Dham) → Transit Southward.

Darshan at Baidyanath Jyotirlinga in Deoghar. Sacred rituals at the ancient temple complex where Ravana revered Lord Shiva. Cross-country transit toward Andhra Pradesh.

07

Days 20–22: Andhra Pradesh & Srisailam

Route: Vijayawada → Srisailam (Nallamala Forest).

Transit through the scenic Krishna river valley to Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga. Darshan of both Jyotirlinga and Bhramaramba Shakti Peetha at the historic hill sanctuary.

08

Days 23–25: Tamil Nadu, Rameshwaram & Return

Route: Madurai → Pamban Bridge → Rameshwaram → Return to Bengaluru.

Cross the ocean Pamban bridge to Rameshwaram Island. Theertha snana in 22 sacred wells followed by darshan at Rameshwaram Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga. Return drive to Bengaluru marking the successful conclusion of the 12 Jyotirlinga Darshana Yatra.

*Note: Exact daily road halts, pooja schedules, and route sequences are subject to prevailing highway regulations, seasonal weather, and local temple timings confirmed prior to departure.
