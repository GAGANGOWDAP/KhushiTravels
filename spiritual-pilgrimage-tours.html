Day-by-Day Journey Plan

Proposed 25-Day Comprehensive Itinerary

A balanced 25-day roadmap starting and ending in Bengaluru, structured with mandatory rest intervals, dual driver shifts, and planned visits across 5 geographic phases.

Phase 1

Maharashtra & Gujarat Circuit (Days 1–6)

Covering Bhimashankar, Trimbakeshwar, Grishneshwar, Somnath, and Nageshwar Jyotirlingas.

D1 Day 1: Departure from Bengaluru • Transit towards Western Ghats
↓

Route: Bengaluru → Tumakuru → Hubballi → Belagavi → Maharashtra border.

Morning departure from Bengaluru in comfortable AC Volvo coach. Scheduled meal stops and regular driver change rotation. Overnight halt en route to ensure passenger freshness for the Sahyadri hill ascent.

D2 Day 2: Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga Darshan • Drive to Nashik
↓

Highlights: Scenic Sahyadri range drive → Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga darshan • Evening transit to Nashik.

Morning darshan at sacred Bhimashankar located in the verdant Sahyadri hills. Afternoon journey towards Nashik with scenic countryside views. Overnight stay in Nashik.

D3 Day 3: Trimbakeshwar & Grishneshwar Jyotirlingas • Gujarat Transit
↓

Highlights: Trimbakeshwar (Godavari origin) darshan → Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga near Ellora → Evening highway transit.

Early morning darshan of the unique three-faced linga at Trimbakeshwar. Proceed to Grishneshwar, the 12th Jyotirlinga. Evening highway travel towards Gujarat border with overnight stay.

D4 Day 4: Saurashtra Coastal Highway • Evening Aarti at Somnath
↓

Highlights: Journey across Gujarat to Prabhas Patan → Somnath Jyotirlinga on Arabian Sea coast.

Scenic coastal highway drive into Saurashtra. Arrive in Somnath by late afternoon. Witness the magnificent evening Sandhya Aarti and light-and-sound show on the seashore. Overnight stay in Somnath.

D5 Day 5: Nageshwar Jyotirlinga • Dwarkadhish • Transit to Central Gujarat
↓

Highlights: Somnath morning darshan → Nageshwar Jyotirlinga (Dwarka) → Dwarkadhish Temple glimpse.

Early morning pooja at Somnath. Proceed along the coastal corridor to Nageshwar Jyotirlinga near Dwarka. Complete evening darshan and begin transit towards central Gujarat. Overnight stay in Rajkot / Jamnagar area.

D6 Day 6: Transit to Madhya Pradesh Corridor • Safe Driver Rest Day
↓

Route: Central Gujarat → Vadodara → Godhra → Madhya Pradesh border.

Dedicated recovery travel day planned specifically to avoid fatigue on long distances and ensure safe driver rotation before entering the Malwa & Narmada pilgrimage belt. Overnight stay near Indore/Omkareshwar gateway.

Phase 2

Central India & The Braj Region (Days 7–10)

Omkareshwar, Mahakaleshwar, plus planned spiritual stops at Mathura & Vrindavan.

D7 Day 7: Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga (Narmada River) • Drive to Ujjain
↓

Highlights: Holy Narmada River boat crossing / bridge to Mandhata Island • Omkareshwar & Mamleshwar Jyotirlinga darshan.

Darshan at the sacred island shaped like Om on the Narmada River. Afternoon departure to holy Ujjain. Evening visit to Ram Ghat along the Shipra River. Overnight stay in Ujjain.

D8 Day 8: Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga (Ujjain) • Transit to Braj Corridor
↓

Highlights: Shri Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga (Dakshinabhimukhi) darshan • Harsiddhi Shakti Peeth • Delhi-Mumbai Expressway transit.

Morning darshan at Mahakaleshwar. Afternoon journey northwards via the expressway towards the sacred Braj region. Overnight stay near Mathura / Agra corridor.

D9 Day 9: Mathura & Vrindavan Sacred Visits (Planned Spiritual Highlights)
↓

Highlights: Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi (Mathura) • Banke Bihari & Prem Mandir (Vrindavan) • Planned stop, subject to time and local conditions.

Morning visit to Mathura's Krishna Janmabhoomi temple complex. Proceed to Vrindavan for Banke Bihari darshan and the exquisite white marble Prem Mandir. Overnight stay in Vrindavan/Mathura.

D10 Day 10: Departure from Braj • Journey to Haridwar (Uttarakhand Gateway)
↓

Highlights: Highway travel to Uttarakhand • Evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri, Haridwar (subject to arrival time).

Smooth expressway journey northward through Western UP into the Himalayan foothills of Haridwar. Holy dip in the Ganga and evening aarti at Har Ki Pauri if arrival permits. Overnight stay in Haridwar.

Phase 3

Uttarakhand & Kedarnath Himalayas (Days 11–14)

Ascent through Garhwal Himalayas to Shri Kedarnath Jyotirlinga and return descent.

D11 Day 11: Haridwar to Guptkashi / Sitapur Base Camp
↓

Route: Haridwar → Rishikesh → Devprayag (Sangam) → Rudraprayag → Guptkashi / Sitapur.

Scenic mountain drive alongside the holy Mandakini River. Brief stop at Devprayag to view the confluence of Bhagirathi and Alaknanda. Acclimatization and rest at Sitapur base camp. Overnight stay.

D12 Day 12: Gaurikund to Kedarnath Jyotirlinga • Evening Sandhya Aarti
↓

Highlights: Sonprayag to Gaurikund local shuttle → Trek to Kedarnath (16 km) / optional pony, doli or helicopter booked directly by pilgrims (not included in package).

Reach sacred Kedarnath Dham surrounded by snow-clad Himalayan peaks. Attend divine evening Sandhya Aarti outside the ancient temple. Overnight stay at Kedarnath Dham / base camp as arranged.

D13 Day 13: Shri Kedarnath Morning Darshan • Descent to Sitapur Base Camp
↓

Highlights: Early morning Darshan of Kedarnath Jyotirlinga • Descent to Gaurikund and Sitapur.

Early morning pooja and darshan of the Swayambhu lingam at 3,583m elevation. Descend to Gaurikund and reunite at Sitapur base camp for rest and hot satvik meals. Overnight stay in Sitapur/Guptkashi.

D14 Day 14: Himalayan Descent to Haridwar • Pilgrim Rest & Recovery
↓

Route: Guptkashi → Srinagar (Garhwal) → Rishikesh → Haridwar.

Scenic descent from the Garhwal Himalayas to the plains of Haridwar. Dedicated recovery evening for senior citizens and pilgrims following the mountain trek. Overnight stay in Haridwar.

Phase 4

Uttar Pradesh, Bihar & Jharkhand (Days 15–19)

Ayodhya, Kashi Vishwanath, Gaya, Bodh Gaya, and Baidyanath Dham.

D15 Day 15: Haridwar to Ayodhya • Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan
↓

Highlights: Highway journey to Ayodhya • Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir & Hanuman Garhi • Planned stop, subject to time and local conditions.

Drive through central UP highways into holy Ayodhya. Visit the majestic Ram Janmabhoomi temple and seek blessings at Hanuman Garhi. Saryu riverfront glimpse. Overnight stay in Ayodhya.

D16 Day 16: Ayodhya to Sacred Kashi (Varanasi) • Evening Ganga Aarti
↓

Route: Ayodhya → Sultanpur → Varanasi (approx. 4.5 hours drive).

Morning departure to the world's oldest living spiritual city, Kashi. Afternoon check-in. Evening boat ride on the sacred Ganga and viewing of the world-famous Maha Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat. Overnight stay in Varanasi.

D17 Day 17: Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga • Transit to Gaya
↓

Highlights: Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple Corridor • Annapurna Temple • Kal Bhairav Temple • Afternoon transit to Gaya.

Early morning darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga through the grand corridor. Afternoon drive into Bihar towards the sacred city of Gaya. Overnight stay in Gaya / Bodh Gaya.

D18 Day 18: Gaya (Vishnupad) & Bodh Gaya (Mahabodhi) • Drive to Deoghar
↓

Highlights: Vishnupad Temple (Gaya) • Mahabodhi Temple Complex & Bodhi Tree (Bodh Gaya) • Planned visits, subject to time and local conditions.

Morning prayers at Vishnupad Temple in Gaya. Visit the historic Mahabodhi Temple and sacred Bodhi Tree in Bodh Gaya. Afternoon journey across Jharkhand towards Baba Baidyanath Dham. Overnight stay in Deoghar.

D19 Day 19: Baidyanath Jyotirlinga (Deoghar) • Transit towards Coastal Odisha
↓

Highlights: Shri Baidyanath Jyotirlinga (Kamana Linga) darshan in Deoghar • Highway transit southward.

Early morning darshan and jalabhisheka at Baba Baidyanath temple in Deoghar. Begin journey southwards towards Odisha via the national highway corridor. Safe overnight halt in transit.

Phase 5

Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu & Return (Days 20–25)

Puri Jagannath, Mallikarjuna (Srisailam), Ramanathaswamy (Rameshwaram) and return to Bengaluru.

D20 Day 20: Arrival in Puri • Shri Jagannath Temple Darshan (Planned Stop)
↓

Highlights: Shri Jagannath Temple darshan in Puri • Sacred ocean beach • Konark view (if schedule allows) • Planned stop, subject to time and local conditions.

Arrive in the holy Dham of Puri. Seek divine blessings of Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra. Experience the sacred Anand Bazaar. Overnight stay in Puri / Brahmapur area.

D21 Day 21: Coastal NH-16 Transit • Journey towards Srisailam Foothills
↓

Route: Odisha → Visakhapatnam corridor → Vijayawada → Kurnool / Dornala gateway.

Comfortable highway transit along the eastern coast towards Andhra Pradesh. Dual driver rotation and passenger meal breaks. Overnight stay at the foothills of the Nallamala range.

D22 Day 22: Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga (Srisailam, AP) • Southward Travel
↓

Highlights: Scenic Nallamala forest ascent → Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga & Bhramaramba Devi Shakti Peeth darshan on the Krishna River.

Ascend through the scenic wildlife sanctuary to Srisailam. Complete darshan at the holy shrine where both Jyotirlinga and Shakti Peeth coexist. Evening transit southwards. Overnight stay in Kadapa / Chittoor area.

D23 Day 23: Transit across Tamil Nadu towards Rameshwaram Island
↓

Route: Andhra border → Vellore → Tiruchirappalli → Ramanathapuram → Pamban Bridge → Rameshwaram.

Travel through Tamil Nadu towards the southern tip of India. Cross the iconic Pamban Bridge over the sea onto Rameshwaram Island. Evening check-in and peaceful rest. Overnight stay in Rameshwaram.

D24 Day 24: Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga Darshan • 22 Theertham Bath • Dhanushkodi
↓

Highlights: Holy dip at Agni Theertham • 22 Kund snan • Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga (longest pillared corridor) • Ram Setu viewpoint at Dhanushkodi.

Sacred sea bath and the traditional 22 temple wells bath inside Ramanathaswamy Temple. Divine darshan of the Shivalinga consecrated by Lord Shri Ram. Afternoon visit to Dhanushkodi beach. Overnight stay in Rameshwaram / Madurai.

D25 Day 25: Return Journey to Bengaluru • Yatra Conclusion
↓

Route: Rameshwaram / Madurai → Dindigul → Salem → Dharmapuri → Hosur → Bengaluru.

Farewell devotional prayers. Return highway journey to Bengaluru with scheduled food and refreshment halts. Arrive in Bengaluru by late evening with hearts filled with the divine grace of all 12 Jyotirlingas and India's greatest sacred shrines.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding the proposed 25-day pan-India pilgrimage.

What is the duration of the complete 12 Jyotirlinga tour?

The proposed package duration is 25 Days / 24 Nights starting and concluding in Bengaluru. The schedule is structured across five geographic phases with built-in rest stops and driver rotation to ensure passenger comfort. Final duration and departure schedules are subject to operator review and confirmation.

Are all 12 Jyotirlingas covered in this single journey?

Yes, the planned itinerary covers all 12 sacred Jyotirlingas: Somnath, Mallikarjuna (Srisailam), Mahakaleshwar, Omkareshwar, Kedarnath, Bhimashankar, Kashi Vishwanath, Trimbakeshwar, Baidyanath (Deoghar), Nageshwar, Ramanathaswamy (Rameshwaram), and Grishneshwar.

How are Mathura, Vrindavan, Ayodhya, Gaya, Bodh Gaya and Puri included?

These six sacred destinations are scheduled as planned spiritual stops along the geographic travel route. They are visited whenever time, highway traffic conditions, and local darshan regulations comfortably permit, serving as enriching pilgrimage highlights without replacing any of the core 12 Jyotirlinga darshans.

What is the confirmed price for this pilgrimage package?

Please contact Khushi Tours & Travels directly for the confirmed package price. Package costs depend on coach category (e.g. AC Volvo multi-axle), double-sharing room preferences, and group size. We do not publish unverified package rates to ensure total pricing integrity.

Are helicopter, doli, or pony charges for Kedarnath included?

No. Helicopter/chopper tickets, doli/palanquin charges, and horse/pony fares are strictly excluded from the base package. Because these services are governed by strict biometric registrations and variable passenger preferences, pilgrims arrange these directly or via our tour coordinator's guidance at actual cost.

What kind of food and accommodation is provided?

The package includes 3-star deluxe hotel rooms on a comfortable double-sharing basis with attached bathrooms and hot water. Three daily wholesome pure vegetarian meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) prepared under hygienic satvik standards are fully covered in the confirmed package.
