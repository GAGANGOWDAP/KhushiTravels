Weekend Getaway⏱ 3 Days / 2 Nights📍 Munnar, Idukki District, Kerala

Munnar Tea Plantations, Mattupetty & Top Station Getaway

Journey into Kerala's most iconic hill station. Marvel at rolling emerald tea carpets, Mattupetty Dam, Kundala Lake, Echo Point, the historic Tea Museum, and panoramic clouds at Top Station.

Book on WhatsAppCall: +91 99013 33113

Trip Highlights

★Scenic tea garden drives and visit to Munnar Tea Museum
★Mattupetty Dam & boat rides on Kundala Lake
★Echo Point & Munnar local market exploration
★Top Station mountain pass with views of Western Ghats

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Verified Schedule
Day 0

Friday Night: Bangalore to Munnar

  • 8:00 PM: Assemble at the designated pickup point in Bangalore.
  • Meet the Trip Captain and fellow travellers.
  • Depart Bangalore by Tempo Traveller.
  • Overnight journey towards Munnar.
Day 1

Saturday: Munnar Exploration

  • Morning arrival in Munnar.
  • Breakfast and check-in / freshen up.
  • Visit Mattupetty Dam & Kundala Lake.
  • Walk through scenic tea gardens.
  • Echo Point & lunch.
  • Explore Munnar town & visit Tea Museum.
  • Sunset at a scenic viewpoint.
  • Return to hotel, dinner, and overnight stay in Munnar.
Stay: Hotel in Munnar
Meals: Breakfast + Dinner
Day 2

Sunday: Munnar to Bangalore

  • Breakfast & check-out.
  • Visit Top Station for breathtaking valley views.
  • Tea plantation drive & optional short nature walk.
  • Lunch & free time / local spice shopping.
  • 3:00–4:00 PM: Depart Munnar for Bangalore.
  • Overnight journey to Bangalore.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 3

Monday Morning: Back in Bangalore

  • 5:00–7:00 AM: Arrive in Bangalore.
  • Trip ends.
