Gokarna Beach Trek, Murudeshwara & Honnavar Backwaters

Trek along the scenic Arabian Sea coastline across Belekan, Paradise, Half Moon, and Om Beach. Includes a group pool party, visit to the colossal Shiva statue at Murudeshwara Temple, and mangrove exploration at Honnavar.

Book on WhatsAppCall: +91 99013 33113

Trip Highlights

★Coastal beach trek: Belekan to Paradise, Half Moon & Om Beach
★Relaxing resort stay with swimming pool party
★Murudeshwara Temple with towering Shiva statue by the sea
★Scenic Honnavar backwaters and mangrove landscape

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Verified Schedule
Day 0

Bangalore to Gokarna (Overnight Journey)

  • Report at the designated pickup point in Bangalore at 9:30 PM.
  • Meet the fellow travellers and Trip Captain.
  • Begin the overnight journey to Gokarna.
Day 1

Beach Trekking, Sunset & Pool Party

Belekan Beach → Paradise Beach → Half Moon Beach → Om Beach → Resort
  • Arrive in Gokarna at dawn.
  • Check in to the resort and freshen up.
  • Have breakfast and relax/explore the resort surroundings.
  • Around 1–2 PM, begin the trek from Belekan Beach.
  • Trek along the coastal trail to Paradise Beach.
  • Continue towards Half Moon Beach.
  • Finally reach Om Beach.
  • Spend the afternoon café-hopping, collecting shells and enjoying the coastline.
  • Return to the resort and relax.
  • Pool party with the group.
Stay: Gokarna resort (4-sharing AC rooms)
Meals: Breakfast
Day 2

Murudeshwara & Honnavar to Bangalore

Murudeshwara Temple → Honnavar Backwaters → Bangalore
  • Breakfast at the resort.
  • Check out.
  • Drive to Murudeshwara Temple.
  • See the massive Shiva statue surrounded by the Arabian Sea on three sides.
  • Continue to Honnavar.
  • Enjoy the scenic Honnavar backwaters and mangrove surroundings (boat ride optional/excluded).
  • Later, depart for Bangalore.
  • Overnight journey back to Bangalore.
Meals: Breakfast
Day 3

Arrival in Bangalore

  • Arrive in Bangalore and conclude the trip.
